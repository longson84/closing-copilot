The solution

Four jobs, one copilot.

Close

Reads IDs, OTPs, S&Ps and receipts; cross-checks them; tracks every legal deadline.

Capture

Turns WhatsApp and profile screenshots into a CRM record with dated follow-ups.

Match

Recommends projects from a catalog of 24 real 2025–26 launches.

Care

After-sales: family & important dates (with consent), tenancy, furnishing, tax reminders.

Documents that check themselves.

  • NRIC photo → Document AI OCR → Gemini extraction → customer + due-diligence record (13 s in our test, tilted phone photo)
  • S&P uploaded → caught “TAN WEI MIN” vs “TAN WEI MING” and two swapped NRIC digits before signing
  • Gemini reads; deterministic code compares — every flag is explainable
  • NRIC masked outside the customer record; ID image not stored (PDPA data minimisation)
